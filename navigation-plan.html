2. Breakpoint evidence

Method and widths tested: Lowered Media query threshold to 100px. Resized until failure was seen, then incremented by 1 pixel until failure resolved. Tested widths: 260-3578px

First content failure: 839px - Brand Link and Nav links collide and both wrapped to a second line.

Chosen breakpoint in relative units: 52.5rem (which is exactly 840px - the exact minimum stable width).

Long-label and zoom retest: Doubled length of three Nav links. New failure was observed at 1086px. Modified media query to trigger at 68rem.
